# Ironhold Production Art Request

## Needed First

- Runtime export: `assets/production_art/tilesets/ironhold_tileset.png`
- Source slot: `assets/art_sources/tilesets/ironhold/`
- Format: PNG with transparency where needed
- Grid: 32 px top-down visual tile family
- Preferred sheet size: at least 512x512

## Must Include

- calm industrial traversal floor and road tiles
- riveted edge transitions and road shoulders
- brick or plate wall-base support
- forge/training apron tiles
- mine threshold, pipe base, crate base, and ore-cart support tiles
- palette separation that lets NPC labels stay legible

## Must Avoid

- icon sheets presented as tilesets
- equally noisy metal texture across the entire route
- large block facades with no base/shadow/road relationship

## Review Checks

- player and Seraphina read clearly over the walkable floor
- training-yard farming prompt remains visible
- existing region collision and travel gates remain untouched
